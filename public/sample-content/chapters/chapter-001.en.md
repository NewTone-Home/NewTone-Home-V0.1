# Chapter One: A Letter from the Valley

<!-- block:ch1-opening -->

Night rose slowly from the valley. The old station clock was the first thing to sink into shadow, frozen at nine seventeen like an eye that had forgotten how to close.

<!-- block:ch1-river -->

The river curved around the birches and scattered silver across the stones. People in town said the market woke only when moonlight touched the third boundary marker.

<!-- world-cue:surface-night -->

<!-- block:ch1-footsteps -->

Footsteps stopped behind him. The courier offered no letter, only a brass clasp engraved with two concentric rings.

<!-- block:ch1-letter -->

“If you still remember the way back, find the door without a shadow before the clock begins again.”

> The ink was fresh, but the paper smelled of dust from years ago.

<!-- block:ch1-market -->

Far away, lamps woke one by one. The visible streets drew apart and an older road surfaced between them.

<!-- world-cue:market-awake -->

<!-- block:ch1-gate -->

He pocketed the clasp and stepped down from the platform. With every step, another faint echo answered from deep inside the valley.

<!-- block:ch1-end -->

The final train was long gone, yet the rails still trembled as if something were arriving from a place no one could see.
